# Excel Expenses Dashboard | FY2025

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **FY2025 Expenses Dashboard built entirely in Microsoft Excel**.

The dashboard was designed to provide a clear view of organizational spending, budget utilization, departmental expenses, payment methods, monthly spending trends, transaction activity, and approval performance.

The goal was to transform raw expense data into an easy-to-use management dashboard that can support financial monitoring and day-to-day business decisions.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations need to monitor expenses closely to understand where money is being spent, how actual spending compares with the available budget, and whether spending patterns require attention.

A simple table of transactions can make it difficult for management to quickly identify:

- Which departments have the highest expenses
- How much of the allocated budget has been used
- How much budget remains
- Which expense categories account for the largest share of spending
- How expenses change throughout the year
- Which payment methods are used most frequently
- How many transactions have been approved
- Whether spending patterns require further investigation

This dashboard addresses these questions by presenting the information in a visual and interactive format.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Expense by Department

The dashboard compares spending across six departments:

- Engineering
- Finance
- HR
- Marketing
- Operations
- Sales

This allows users to quickly identify departments with higher or lower spending.

### 2. Expense by Category

A category-level breakdown shows how total expenses are distributed across different expense categories, including:

- Consulting
- Equipment
- Marketing
- Office Supplies
- Software
- Training

This provides visibility into the major areas contributing to overall spending.

### 3. Monthly Expense Trend

The monthly trend visual shows how expenses changed throughout FY2025.

The analysis helps identify months with relatively high or low spending and provides a basis for investigating unusual changes in expenditure.

### 4. Budget vs Actual

The Budget vs Actual chart compares departmental spending against allocated budgets.

This helps management monitor budget utilization and identify departments where actual expenses are approaching or exceeding planned spending.

### 5. Payment Method

Expenses are also analyzed by payment method:

- Credit Card
- Cheque
- Cash
- Bank Transfer

This provides visibility into how organizational expenses are being paid.

### 6. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

- Month
- Department

Users can select one or multiple values to dynamically filter the dashboard and examine specific areas of spending.

## Key Findings

Based on the dashboard:

### Overall Spending

Total expenses were approximately **$18.4 million**, against an allocated budget of approximately **$23.0 million**.

The dashboard shows approximately **$4.55 million in remaining budget**.

### Departmental Spending

The highest departmental expenses shown in the dashboard were:

- HR: approximately $3.62M
- Operations: approximately $3.35M
- Engineering: approximately $3.37M
- Marketing: approximately $3.01M

Finance and Sales recorded comparatively lower spending.

### Monthly Spending

Spending varied throughout the year.

The highest monthly expense shown was approximately **$2.12M in July**, while February had the lowest monthly expense at approximately **$1.06M**.

This type of monthly analysis can help identify periods requiring additional investigation or budget planning.

### Payment Methods

Cheque, bank transfer, and cash accounted for substantial portions of total spending, while credit card payments represented a smaller amount relative to the other payment methods shown.

### Approval Performance

The dashboard shows an **81.4% approval rate** across 415 transactions.

This provides a useful operational measure for monitoring the status of expense transactions.
